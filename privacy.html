Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nyx ♡ NSU (“Nyx”) may process information when you use the bot in Discord.

1. Information Nyx May Process

Nyx may receive basic Discord information required to provide bot features, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, usernames or display names, command interactions, and information you intentionally provide through bot commands.

2. Current Data Storage

The current starter version of Nyx does not intentionally maintain a separate long-term database of user profiles or message history. Information used for commands is generally processed only as needed to respond to the interaction.

3. Future Features

If Nyx later adds moderation logs, leveling, tickets, settings, reminders, or other features that require stored data, this Privacy Policy should be updated to describe what is stored, why it is stored, and how long it is retained.

4. Sharing of Information

Nyx does not sell personal information. Information may be handled by Discord and any hosting provider used to run the bot as necessary to provide the service.

5. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect bot credentials and data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You can stop using Nyx at any time. Server owners may also remove Nyx from their server. For questions about data associated with Nyx, contact Night Stalkers Unit staff or the bot owner.

7. Discord

Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s own policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Nyx’s features or data practices change.
